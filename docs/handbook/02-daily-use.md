# Part 2: Daily use

## Conversations

Each conversation is a thread.
Up to three threads can answer at the same time.
If you write while a thread is answering, your message waits as "queued" and goes next; you can cancel it.
Threads can be renamed, pinned and archived.
Archive hides a thread; nothing is deleted, and the brain keeps its record.

To point at part of an answer, click a word: the phrase around it is underlined, and "note" lets you attach a remark to your next message.

## Files

The Files pane opens beside the conversation when the reasoner makes something.
It has five places: out (what the reasoner made), in (what you attached), work (your repositories, if set up), world (a read-only copy of your files, if set up), and brain (the brain's own code).
Audio and video play, images and documents show, anything downloads, and "ask the brain" puts the file into the conversation.

## Memos

Memos are not part of the standard brain.
If the owner of your server added them, they will show you where they live; otherwise skip this.

## Apps and connections

Brain-apps are small programs that live in your brain, on the Apps page.
When you install one you approve which parts of your memory it may read; it cannot read local-only memory.
On the Integrations page you can connect email (with an app password, not your real one), a calendar link, and web research.
The brain reads them and treats what they say as information, never as orders.
Writing to a connected app (sending an email, creating an event) always shows a card first.

## Language

In Settings, the Language block has two choices and one slider.
Answer language: by default the brain answers in the language you write in; pick one to fix it (English, Russian, German, Slovak, Spanish, French, Italian, Ukrainian, or "other" and write the name).
Language I am learning: off by default; pick one and the brain answers in that language, then repeats the same answer under a thin rule in your own language, so you read both.
Balance: how much of the answer is in the language you are learning, from 0 (a sentence or two) to 100 (everything); 50 to start.
These two settings win over anything the persona says about language; the Persona page says so in one line.
Saving takes effect on the next message; the settings stay through Update. (2026-10-25)

## Update

The Update page has one button, "Pull latest".
It fetches the newest version of the brain's software, rebuilds it, and checks that it came back.
Your memory, conversations, persona and settings stay.
During the rebuild the page loses its connection for a while and then waits up to about three minutes for the new version.
Press it once and wait for the green or red banner; a second press does not make it faster.
If you would rather not think about it, switch on "Keep this brain updated": once a day, at the hour you choose, it does the same thing when there is something new.
