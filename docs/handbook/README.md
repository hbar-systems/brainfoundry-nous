# Member Handbook

This is the handbook for people who have a brain.
It is written for you if you are not technical.
Each part is one page; the deep detail is in the documents each part links to.

You can read it here, on GitHub, or in your console: open Guide, then the Handbook tab.

## The parts

1. [Day one](01-day-one.md): log in, the first conversation, the first documents, the phone.
2. [Daily use](02-daily-use.md): conversations, files, apps, and the Update button.
3. [Workers](03-workers.md): standing agents, one-off jobs, helpers, and what may run without asking you.
4. [Memory and privacy](04-memory-and-privacy.md): what is remembered, who reads it, export and backup.
5. [Other brains](05-other-brains.md): federation, the ask door, People.
6. [Costs](06-costs.md): billed, plan and cap; where spend shows; how to set a ceiling.
7. [Safety](07-safety.md): the three gates you keep, and what to do if a key leaks.
8. [Under the hood](08-under-the-hood.md): where the technical documents are.

## Three words used throughout

The **brain** is your server: its memory, its persona, its console.
The **reasoner** is the model that answers you and does the work; it runs on your server, on your own account.
A **card** is the small box in the conversation that shows one exact action and waits for you to press Allow (or Send) or refuse.

Where this handbook and the code disagree, the code is right; please report the difference.
