# Part 6: Costs

Running a brain costs three kinds of things: the server, the models, and your attention.
This page is about the models, which you pay to their provider directly, on your own account.

## Billed, plan and cap

**Billed** means you pay per use: with an API key, every answer, job and agent run is charged to your provider account.
**Plan** means a subscription: you pay a fixed plan, and the amounts the console shows are what the same work would cost if paid per use, not a bill.
If your plan lets you buy extra usage past its limit, that extra part is billed.
**Cap** means a limit you choose; the brain has one for its own spending, and your provider usually has one too.

## Where spend shows

Settings, Spending: today, the last 7 days and the last 30 days, in euros (converted from dollars at a fixed rate).
On a subscription it says so under the numbers.
Each Board tile shows the cost of that work today.
Each agent shows its budget per run on the Agents page.
Some costs are not known to the brain: a job records how long it ran, not what it cost.

## Setting a ceiling

1. In Settings, Spending, type a limit for 30 days and press Save; "No limit" takes it away.
2. Over that limit, scheduled agents do not start; "run now" on the Agents page still works, because that press is yours.
3. Give each agent a small budget per run; a run stops when it reaches its budget.
4. Set a spending limit in your provider account as well.

The brain's limit stops agents only; your conversations and jobs keep running past it.
The provider's limit is the one that stops everything.

What anyone charges you for running your server is agreed outside the brain and is not shown here.
