Join my brain

This puts your laptop on your brain's own private network, so your brain can help you
sort what is on it. In order:

1. Tailscale, a private network app, is installed if you do not have it (macOS asks for
   your password once). It connects only to your brain, never to anyone else's.
2. You turn on Remote Login for your own user (System Settings opens at the right page).
3. One line is added that lets your brain run one small program here, door.sh, and
   nothing else. Every time it does, a line is written to ~/.brain-spoke/door.log.
4. "Leave my brain" appears on your Desktop. Double-click it any time to undo all of this.

First your brain only LOOKS: how many photos, documents, songs, how big, how old.
No file names leave the laptop for that. It never opens your passwords (keychain),
your messages, your mail or your browsers. It moves nothing until you say yes, in the
conversation, to a plan you have read. Nothing is ever deleted.
